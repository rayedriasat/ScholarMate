# Source Selection Persistence - Quick Reference

## Overview
The AI chat now remembers which documents you've selected as sources, so you don't have to reselect them every time you open the chat.

## How It Works

### Automatic Saving
Your source selections are saved automatically whenever you:
- ✅ Check or uncheck a document
- ✅ Click "Select All"
- ✅ Click "Clear All"

No manual save button needed!

### Automatic Loading
When you open the AI chat screen:
- ✅ Your previous selections are restored
- ✅ Selected documents are highlighted
- ✅ Selection count is displayed
